# Syncopation Pro — Privacy Policy

**Syncopation Pro collects no data. None.**

- The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking.
- Your music, your files, and your iPod's contents never leave your Mac.
- One feature reaches the internet, and only when you use it. Synco-cair
  (from version 1.2) looks up missing album covers and album names. It sends
  album, artist, and track names from your own files as search terms — and,
  when a name isn't enough, an audio signature — to public music databases
  (Apple's catalog through MusicKit or the iTunes Search API, Deezer,
  MusicBrainz, Cover Art Archive, and Apple's ShazamKit) over HTTPS. No
  account, no identifiers, no device information. MusicKit and ShazamKit
  work under macOS's Media & Apple Music permission; Syncopation only looks
  things up in Apple's public catalog and never reads or changes your Apple
  Music library. Nothing is retained by the developer. A switch in Settings,
  **Prevent Syncopation from Internet Access**, turns all of it off; nothing
  else in the app has ever needed the network.
- Everything the app reads — your music folder and the iPod you connect —
  is accessed only with permission you grant through macOS, used only for
  what you asked, and stored nowhere else.
- The only things the app writes are the files and database on the
  destination you chose, the names, tags and cover art that Synco-cair puts
  right in your own files and the folders it files them into (the audio
  itself is never re-encoded), and its own records on your Mac: your folder
  paths and settings, the library index, Cair's log of what it changed, and
  Auditoré's loudness measurements.

There is nothing to opt out of, because nothing is collected. If you would
rather the app never touch the network at all, flip the switch.
