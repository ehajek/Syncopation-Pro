# Syncopation Pro — Privacy Policy

**Syncopation Pro collects no data. None.**

- The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking.
- It makes no network connections. Your music, your files, and your iPod's
  contents never leave your Mac.
- Everything the app reads — your music folder and the iPod you connect —
  is accessed only with permission you grant through macOS, used only to
  perform the sync you asked for, and stored nowhere else.
- The only things the app writes are the files and database on the
  destination you chose, and its own preferences (your folder paths and
  settings) on your Mac.

There is nothing to opt out of, because nothing is collected.
